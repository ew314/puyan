Privacy Policy

Puyan Teleprompter · 2026-11-25

The short version

Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing is uploaded.

If you delete the app, the scripts, settings and reading positions it stored go with it. One thing stays behind in the iOS keychain: an anonymous timestamp of the moment your free three days began. It exists so that reinstalling cannot restart the free days. It carries no name and no identifier, and it never leaves the device.

Microphone

The microphone is opened only while voice following is on. You start it, you stop it.

Audio is decoded in memory on the device to work out which word you are on. It is not written to a file and it does not leave the device.

Camera and recordings

The camera is opened only when you start a recording.

Recordings are saved to your own photo library. We never receive a copy.

Your scripts and settings

Scripts, settings and reading positions are stored inside the app on your device.

There is no cloud copy, so there is nothing for us to hand over, sell, or lose.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We are told only whether a purchase is active — never your name, card, or address.

No tracking

There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporter in the app.

We do not build a profile of you and we do not follow you across other apps or websites.

Children, your rights, and changes

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to export or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device; the single trial timestamp in the keychain, described above, is the only thing that stays.

If this policy changes, the date at the foot of this page changes with it.
